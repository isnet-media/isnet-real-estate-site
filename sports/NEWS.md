## Research and original writing
Research all accessible Israeli sports outlets through broad searches plus ONE, Sport5, Doublepass, VOLE, Walla, Sport1/Maariv, ynet, Israel Hayom, Haaretz and Isport. Cover all Ashdod teams and local athletes, adults/women/youth, keeping team identity distinct. This registry is an intended source list, not proof all outlets were scanned.
Inspect actual publication timestamps and article contents; page clocks and crawl dates don't establish freshness. Compare events against manifest event_key and existing URLs. Prioritize the last 48 hours. The first broad searches on 15 October returned historical pages as well as current results; don't republish old events as new.
Use a specific official source or two independently reported matching sources for a routine news item. Match reports and tables come from football.org.il, basket.co.il, ibasketball.co.il and handballisr.co.il by sport. Sensitive injury/personal/allegation stories need an appropriate explicit official statement for unattended publication. Single-outlet unconfirmed claims should not be automatically published as fact.
Write original structure and local angle using verified facts; don't closely paraphrase a full source or fabricate quotations, interviews or eyewitness reporting. Attribute reporting and link exact sources. Distinguish calculations and analysis from facts. No new verified development means no new story or empty-hour commit.
Update the same event/story when warranted instead of duplicating it. coverage entries must distinguish full read, search discovery only, blocked and failed. Never mark configured sources as successfully scanned; do not change verified timestamps on failures. No bypassing access blocks.

Verify the actual credit and origin. Do not reuse other sports outlets' pictures. Record exact photo source and credit, label archive accurately.
